About

    

      

        I'm a DevOps engineer with close to a year of hands-on experience, currently working out of Hyderabad. My work sits at the join between infrastructure and automation — provisioning cloud resources on AWS, containerizing applications with Docker, and running them on Kubernetes.
      

      

        Ansible is where the automation lives: configuration that stays consistent whether it's touching one server or fifty. On the access side, I work with IAM to keep permissions scoped to what's actually needed, and with S3 and EC2 as the everyday building blocks of the infrastructure I manage.
      

      

        Early career, but the habits are already there: read the logs before you guess, automate the second time you do something by hand, and never deploy on a Friday without a rollback plan.
